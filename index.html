About Us

Built on a Vision for Emerging Technology

BrainChild Innovation was founded in Oakland, California with a simple but powerful idea — harness Mixed Reality and artificial intelligence to solve real enterprise problems.

That vision evolved into a proprietary platform for AI-powered visual infrastructure inspection, combining machine learning, computer vision, and cloud infrastructure to deliver actionable insight at scale.

Today, headquartered in San Jose, CA, we specialize in custom AI workstations, generative AI models, and enterprise solutions tailored to unique business challenges.
